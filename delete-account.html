How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below:

  1. Send an email to: yogeshweb18@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include your registered email address or phone number.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order history
  • Vendor profile data (if applicable)

Data That May Be Retained

  • Transaction records for legal and tax purposes
  • Records required by applicable laws

Retention Period: Certain records may be retained for up to 90 days or as required by law.

Once verified, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted.
