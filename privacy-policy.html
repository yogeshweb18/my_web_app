Privacy Policy for Lemon

Effective Date: 01- Mar-2026

Lemon ("we", "our", "us") operates a multi-vendor vegetable marketplace platform that connects customers and vendors for ordering fresh vegetables and groceries.

1. Information We Collect

a) Customer Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Order details

b) Vendor Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Shop name and address
  • Product listings

c) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data
  • Crash logs
  • Firebase-generated identifiers

2. Use of Firebase

We use Google Firebase services such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore/Realtime Database, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics to operate and improve the app.

Firebase may collect device information, usage data, and crash reports. This information is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user and vendor accounts
  • To process and manage orders
  • To connect customers with vendors
  • To improve app performance and fix technical issues
  • To provide customer support

4. Payments

All payments are handled directly between customers and vendors via Cash on Delivery (COD). We do not process or store any credit/debit card or online payment information.

5. Data Sharing

Customer delivery information is shared only with the selected vendor for order fulfillment. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
